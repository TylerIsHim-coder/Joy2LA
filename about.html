About Joy2LA

Bringing People Together. Creating Opportunities to Give. Spreading Joy.

Joy2LA was founded on a simple belief: there are people all around us who want to make a difference—they just need an opportunity to do it.

Our mission is to bring the community and volunteers together by creating meaningful opportunities to give back throughout Los Angeles.

We believe everyone has something to give. It may be your time, your talents, your resources, or simply your willingness to show up. Joy2LA creates ways for people to use what they have to make a meaningful difference in someone else’s life.

Our work takes many forms because the needs of our community do too.

We bring barbers and braiders together to provide free hair services while supplying the hair and products needed to serve our unhoused neighbors. We invest in students through books, Career Day experiences, incentives that reward excellent attendance, and special traditions that support and celebrate their school community. We show up for young mothers, families, and individuals through mentorship, celebrations, and moments of care. And throughout the year, we create community giveback festivals and holiday experiences that bring families, volunteers, and neighbors together.

Some of our programs happen every month. Others have become traditions we return to year after year. And sometimes, we simply recognize a need in our community and find a way to help.

But no matter what the giveback looks like, the heart behind it remains the same:

See a need. Bring people together. Spread joy.

Because to us, giving back is about more than what we provide. It’s about people connecting with people. It’s about making someone feel seen, remembered, supported, and celebrated.

And something beautiful happens when we serve together: the joy doesn’t stop with the person receiving it. It reaches the person giving, too.

That’s Joy2LA.

A community coming together to create more joy—one person, one opportunity, and one act of kindness at a time.
